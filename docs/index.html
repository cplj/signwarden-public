SignWarden

Receipts and audit records for AI agent actions: authorization → action → result. An authorization module of Truquence.

Status: early research. The proposed format and verifier have not been independently validated. This site describes a discussion draft; no verifier or hosted service is shipped.

The problem

An agent may have a permission record, a tool-call log and a separate record of the outcome. The research question is what a receipt must contain for a reviewer to trace an action back to its authorization and compare the requested action with the recorded result.

Receipt fields: discussion draft

These are proposed fields, not a stable specification or a working verification interface.

FieldIntended content
receipt_idIdentifier that links the records for one action.
actorAgent identity and the party on whose behalf it acts.
authorizationIssuer, permitted scope, constraints, validity period and reference to the permission record.
actionTool name, requested operation and reference to the inputs.
resultObserved outcome, error or an explicit unknown result.
timestampsTimes for authorization, execution and observation, with their sources.
evidenceReferences to supporting records and their integrity information.

Whether these fields are sufficient, and which evidence a verifier can check, remain research questions. A recorded claim alone does not establish that permission existed or that an outcome occurred.

Open format, private data

The plan is to open-source the receipt format and reference verifier. Hosted services and stored data remain private.
